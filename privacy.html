Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SLIDEBY (“we”) publishes news videos about international politics. This policy describes how our private publishing tool, “SLIDEBY Publisher”, uses data from Google/YouTube and TikTok.

Who uses the tool

The tool is operated by the owner of the SLIDEBY channel for the owner’s own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What we access

  • YouTube (Google) — YouTube API Services: permission to upload videos to the owner’s channel and to change their visibility (private → public) and thumbnails after the owner approves them.
  • TikTok: the account’s display name and open ID, and permission to send our own videos to that account as drafts or posts.

What we store

Only the OAuth access and refresh tokens, on the owner’s own computer, in files readable only by the owner. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers, subscribers, followers or any other person. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SLIDEBY Publisher’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube API Services, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We share nothing with third parties. Videos are made from official public sources, which are credited in the video descriptions.

Your choices

The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account permissions and in TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services), or ask us to delete the stored tokens.
