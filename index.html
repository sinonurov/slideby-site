SLIDEBY

SLIDEBY is a news channel about international politics. We make short vertical videos and longer news bulletins and documentaries from official government and international-organisation sources, in English.

About this app

“SLIDEBY Publisher” is a private tool used only by the owner of the SLIDEBY channel. It uploads the channel’s own, owner-approved videos to the owner’s own YouTube channel (YouTube API Services) and sends them to the owner’s own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
